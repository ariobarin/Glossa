# Codex ran out. Keep going with ChatGPT.

You've already paid for ChatGPT. When Codex runs out and you've still got ChatGPT usage left, Glossa lets you put it to work on your project.

Connect regular ChatGPT chat to a folder on your computer. ChatGPT can read your code, make edits, and use your development tools with the access you choose.

Keep working in the checkout you already have, with your uncommitted changes and local setup.
